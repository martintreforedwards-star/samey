How to play
  • Each day takes you somewhere in the UK. On most days the map spins and lands on a region, and you get five of its words.
  • Words are asked different ways: pick the real word from two made-up ones, pick what a word means, fill the gap in a sentence, or say where in the region you'd hear it.
  • Wednesday is a theme day: five ways to say one thing from around the country. Place each one on the map.
  • Saturday is tricky: three harder questions. Sunday is the Grand Tour: one word from each of five regions.
  • One round a day. Practice rounds after that don't count.
  • Words are checked against the BBC Voices survey and the Our Dialects maps where we can. Know better? Suggest a word or send feedback below.
