How to play
  • Each day there's one word, name, place or title with its marks taken out: accents like é, ñ and ü, and punctuation like ! ' and -.
  • Tap a mark in the tray, then tap where it goes. Accents go on letters. Punctuation goes in the dashed gaps.
  • Press Check. Marks in the right place turn green and stay. Any in the wrong place come off. Each check uses a go, and you have 4.
  • Stuck? A hint puts one mark in for you, but uses a go.
  • Every day has a twist. Sunday to Tuesday are gentle, then it gets trickier from Wednesday, with Friday the hardest.
  • Solve it in 1 or 2 goes without a hint and you unlock the bonus round: name one of the marks for a bonus star. Sometimes it's a rare one, and it goes into the mark glossary.
