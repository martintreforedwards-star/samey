💡 Got an idea for a game?

Every game here started as a wouldn't it be fun if…? If there's a corner of language you'd love a daily puzzle about, tell me. Half-formed ideas are welcome.

What's already covered?
  • Meanings: synonyms (Samey), -nyms (Spinonym), forgotten words (Passé), local words (Natter), collective nouns (Gaggle), sports words (Gamey)
  • Word histories: etymology (Roots), new words (Rizz), dying words (Dodo), words from fiction (Inkling), other languages (Babel), the tongues of these islands (Mither)
  • Grammar and sound: accents and punctuation (Dotty), grammar (Syntaxed), prepositions (Twixt), odd spellings (Mispelt), prefixes and suffixes (Antepostee), pronunciation (Phoney), rhyme and metre (Rhymey)
  • Books, poems and phrases: first lines (Incipit), poets (Versed), Shakespeare and Chaucer (Forsooth), idioms (Idiomy), similes and metaphors (Doornail)

What I do with your message: privacy.

Thank you!
