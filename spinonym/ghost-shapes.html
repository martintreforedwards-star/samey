Ghost Shapes

Every icon here is a skeuomorph: a design that keeps the look of the thing it replaced, long after the original reason has gone. Name the old thing.

How to play
  • Three icons a day, the same for everyone.
  • Each one was drawn from something older. Type what it was. Close enough counts: "floppy" will do for a floppy disk.
  • You have 4 goes per icon. A hint uses up a go.
  • Every answer comes with the story of why the shape survived.
