    How to play
    • Spin the wheel to find out today's kind of word. Everyone gets the same spin, so you can compare notes.
    • New to a word type? The reveal tells you what it means, and the ? button brings it back mid-puzzle.
    • You have 4 goes. Your guess can be any length, but it has to be a real word. Name days (pseudonyms and exonyms) take any guess.
    • Every letter in your guess that's also in the answer lights up in all its spots, and stays lit.
    • Anagram days are different: use exactly the letters you're given, in any order (it doesn't have to be a real word). Any letter in the right place locks in.
    • Guess another fair answer and we'll tell you it's close. It won't cost a go, and it doesn't check any letters.
    • Stuck? A hint fills in part of the word, but uses up a go.
    • Solve it to unlock the bonus round. Get all three right to find an Easter egg in the glossary.
    • Win 7 days in a row and a golden bonus puzzle unlocks, with a rarer kind of word. It comes round again every 7th day of your streak.
